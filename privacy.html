Privacy Policy for TwinklyPairs

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Overview

TwinklyPairs is a single-player memory matching puzzle game for iOS. The game is designed to be played offline and does not require an account, login, subscription, advertising identifier, or network connection.

Data Collection

TwinklyPairs does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location data, photos, analytics identifiers, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

Local Game Data

The game may save progress, unlocked levels, scores, settings, and star ratings locally on your device using iOS local storage. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

TwinklyPairs does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, social login, or in-app purchase services.

Children's Privacy

TwinklyPairs is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Security

Because TwinklyPairs does not collect or transmit personal information, user data is protected by the security mechanisms provided by iOS and the user's device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy if the app's features change. Any future changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store product page.
