Privacy Policy for TwinklyPairs

Last updated: May 14, 2026

TwinklyPairs is a single-player pair matching puzzle game for iOS. The game is designed to be played offline and does not require an account, login, subscription, advertising identifier, or network connection.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Local Game Data

Game progress, level status, scores, stars, and settings may be stored locally on your device. This information stays on the device and is not sent to the developer or third parties.

Third-Party Services

TwinklyPairs does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, social login, or in-app purchase services.

Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, please contact the developer through the App Store product page or support channel.
